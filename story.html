At Olateam Bakery, we believe that bread is the soul of every meal. Since 1998, we have been perfecting the art of long-fermentation and traditional stone-milling.

Our journey started in a small family kitchen with a single goal: to share the honest, rustic flavors of our heritage with the world. Today, we continue that legacy, one hand-shaped loaf at a time.
