Three generations of bakers, one stubborn belief: good bread takes time, and the best things are made by hand.

Our beginning

Crafted by hand, guided by heart.

Olateam began in a small family kitchen with one oven, a dented mixing bowl, and a starter kept alive in a jam jar. We sold our first loaves to neighbours on a Saturday morning and were sold out by nine.

Almost thirty years later, very little has changed. We still mix by hand, still shape every loaf ourselves, and still believe that bread is the soul of the table.

The journey

A few milestones

1998

The first oven

Olateam opens as a two-person kitchen, baking four loaves at a time for the neighbourhood.

2004

The starter

Our wild levain is born. We have fed it every day since, and it still leavens every loaf.

2012

The bakery

We move into our current home on Bakery Street and add a stone deck oven and pastry bench.

2026

Today

The next generation is at the bench, baking the same recipes with a few new ideas.

What we value

Three things we will not compromise

Patience

Fermentation cannot be faked. We give every dough the time it asks for, even when it makes the day longer.

Provenance

We buy flour from mills we can visit and butter from farms we know. Good bread starts before the mixing bowl.

Generosity

The end of the day's bread goes to neighbours and local kitchens, because food is better shared.

“We are not trying to make the cheapest loaf. We are trying to make the one you think about on the way home.”
Mara Olateam, head baker
