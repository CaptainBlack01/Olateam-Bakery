How we bake

Three days. Three ingredients.

Flour, water, salt — and a starter we have kept alive for more than twenty years. Everything else is just time and heat.

01

Feed the starter

Our wild levain is refreshed twice a day, building the acidity and lift that give each loaf its character.

02

Fold & rest

Dough is hand-folded and rested for two days. No shortcuts, no commercial yeast, no rush.

03

Bake in stone

Heated stone decks and steam give a deep, caramelised crust and an open, custardy crumb.

Our philosophy

Slow fermentation, deeper flavour.

We believe good bread cannot be hurried. Our 48-hour fermentation lets the grain do the talking, breaking down starches and building a flavour that is complex, digestible, and quietly remarkable.

It takes longer. It costs more. We would not have it any other way.
