The bread collection

Golden crust,
open crumb.

Hand-shaped heritage grains, naturally leavened over 48 hours. Each loaf carries the patience of a slow kitchen and the character of good flour.

Good to know

Whole loaves last three days.

Keep your loaf cut-side down at room temperature. To bring back the crust, splash the surface with water and warm it at 200°C for five minutes. Sliced loaves freeze beautifully for up to a month.
